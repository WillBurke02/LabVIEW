INPUTS/OUTPUTS SPECIFICATIONS

 

PRF IN on USPC card

Compatible TTL input loaded with internal pull-up of 4.7 KW to +5V

Low level :        1 mA

High level:   <    1 mA

Fall and rise time < 1 µsec. Without glitch that could trigger random Pulses.

High level must last at least 5 µsec. , Pulse triggering on falling flank of PRF IN.

 

ENABLE on USPC card

Compatible TTL input loaded with internal pull-up of 4.7 KW to +5V

Low level:         1 mA

High level:   <    1 mA

This input enables acquisition when trigger-control acquisition mode is switched on ENABLE condition (see SDK for USPC)

 

START IN on USPC card

Exclusively connecting another USPC card.

 

PRF OUT on USPC board

NB: This output may already be connected to a MUX board; in that case we strongly suggest not connecting this output to other external units.

Open collector loaded to max. +5V, max. 5mA.

Low level max 5 mA

High level » 0 A; it is suggested to use a pull-up resistor min. 470W under 5V, when this output is not loaded by a MUX board, and a resistor min. 1 KW under 5V, when connected to a MUX.

Pulse triggering on falling flank of PRF OUT, high level must last about 5 µsec. and  cycle = PRF.

 

START OUT on USPC card

NB: This output may already be connected to a MUX board, in that case we strongly suggest not to connect this output to other external units.

Open collector loaded to max. +5V, max. 5mA.

Low level max 5 mA

High level » 0 A; it is suggested to use a pull-up resistor min. 470W under 5V, when this output is not loaded by a MUX board, and a resistor min. 1 KW under 5V, when connected to a MUX.

The falling flank of PRF within START OUT low level will trigger the test 1 of cycle of tests.

START OUT goes up a few 100s nsec. after  test 1 PRF falling flank.

START OUT goes down at the end of ultrasonic depth of last active test of cycle of tests.

 

ANALOG OUTPUT

About 0 to 5V with min. 2 KW load, not timed and reset every PRF when using a one channel USPC card, and every cycle of active tests when using a MUX.

 

ON/OFF OUTPUT

Open collector loaded to max. +30V under max. 20mA.

Low level when alarm ON (TOF min., TOF max., alarm amplitude…) max. 20mA.

High level when alarm OFF, » 0 A; it is suggested to use a pull-up resistor min. 470W under 5V, or 2.7 KW under 24V.

ON/OFF outputs are limited out on low level according to a timer set in PCXUS.INI file.

 

 

Connecting tensions directly, without resistors, to Open collector Output Pins can damage output stage of the card

 

IT IS STRONGLY RECOMMANDED TO CONNECT ALL GROUNDS BETWEEN USPC CARD AND EXTERNAL UNIT DIGITAL INPUTS WHO LOAD IT.

RECOMMENDATION FOR CONNECTING WIRES

 

NB: Those connexions must not be disturbed by external environment, so that it is strongly suggested to take all necessary precautions recommended by rules of art.

 

Connexion between USPC cards can be done with flat cable 0.22 mm², max. length 2 meters.

 

Connexion RX (in USPC) to JS1 (OUT MUX or RCPP) must be made with coaxial cable, 50W impedence.

 

Connexion between USPC card and MUX (PRF OUT and START OUT) can be done with flat cable 0.22 mm², max. length 2 meters; for over 2 meters, cables must be shielded twisted with 100W impedance, shield grounded at both ends. Distributed capacity to one meter is £ 100pF when cable is < 30 meters long, and 50pF when up to 60 meters. For longer cables, ask reseller for advice according to written specifications.
