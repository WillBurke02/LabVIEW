MAIN TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

 

USPC 3100 I PCI card

 

Pulser

 

-          square wave

-          power: 125/250 Volts

-          fall-time: 5ns

-          PRR

o        20 to 20 000 Hz internal trigger

o        0 to 20 000 Hz external trigger

-          probe : single or dual crystal

-          pulser frequency : 0.5 to 20 MHz

-          trigger : internal/external

 

Receiver/Amplifier

 

-          Impedance : 50 Ohms

-          Band width: 0.5 to 20 MHz (-3 dB)

-          Gain : 70 dB

-          Second gain active after DAC for back echo calibration +/- 70dB

-          Dynamic : 105 dB (0.1 dB step)

-          Attenuator : 0/15/20 & 35 dB

-          Mode : Pulse echo/Full transmission

-          Filters : 0.5-1-2.25-5-10-15 MHz and wide band

-          True rejection : 0 to 50 %, active on A-scan & Gates

-     RF output: 1.5 Vpp (50 Ohms), 100% HS

 

DAC

-          Dynamic : 70 dB

-          Segments : 30

-          Max. slope: +/- 40 dB/µsec

-          Trigger : Initial pulse, Interface

 

Gates

            - Number : 1 interface tracking & 2 measuring gates, fully independent.

 

-          Measuring Gates

o        Level : 10 to 90% HS(1% step), 2 symmetrical levels in RF signal

o        Trigger : initial pulse, interface, inactive & for gate 2 only : echo gate 1

o        Start & width : 40ns to 655 µs (20 ns step)

o        Alarm : Positive / negative

o        Number of violation before alarm : 1 to 30

o        Measuring modes:

§         Amplitude : first echo, Max. echo

§         TOF/WT : Level, Peak, Zero crossing – 5 nsec resolution in real time.

 

IN/Output connector

o        Analog output :

§         Amplitude : 0 to 4.0 V (100% HS)

§         TOF/WT : 0 to 4.5 V

§         Coupling

 

o        On/Off output:

§         Amplitude, min. TOF/WT, max. TOF/WT, coupling alarms

 

o        Triggers:

§         PRF link

§         PRF In and Out

§         START In and Out

 

o        TX, RX and RF signal subclick connectors.

 

Consumption:

 

            + 12V,  0.5 A

            + 5V,    1.0 A

            + 3.3V, 4.0 A

            - 12V,   0.1 A

 

Operating temperature:

 

            0° to 50° C (32° to 122° F)

 

           

USPC 3100 L

 

Same specifications as USPC 3100 I card.

Transfer by Bus Master mode of A/B and C scan data over PCI bus.

 

USPC 3100 MB

 

Same specifications as USPC 3100 I card, without pulser, and attenuators.

Must be exclusively used with MUX or RCPP boards.

 

MUX. 8 channels/ RCPP 1 channel

 

Remote pulser:

 

            Same specifications as USPC 3100 I card.

            Max. PRF 20 KHz / number of active tests.

 

Preamplifier:

 

            12 dB Gain.

Attenuators; same specifications as USPC 3100 I card.

 

Remote control of pulser and attenuators with RS232 interface

 

Triggers:

 

            PRF IN

            START IN

 

MUX. 8 channels:

 

            One TX and one RX subclick connector per channel.

 

RCPP 1 channel:

 

            One TX and one RX

 

Ascan signal:

 

            Subclick connector. Sequential transfer of up to 8 signals with MUX.

 

Supply:

           

            +5V;     1 A.

            +12V;   1 A.

 

Operating temperature:

 

            0° to 50° C (32° to 122° F)
