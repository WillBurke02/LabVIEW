Main rules

 

  1. One MUX, and only one, is always connected to one USPC 3100 MB or MBC card.

Next connection must always been made up:

 

USPC 3100 MB

MUX 3108

START OUT    K4, way 17

START IN     K2, way 5

PRF OUT        K4, way 21

PRF IN         K2, way 7

RX IN   J2

OUT             JS1

 

  1. When more than one USPC card in one PC, with or without MUX board, we must synchronize them (PRF) as described on here after sketches.

 

  1. When PRF In is being used the cycle of this input signal must be greater than:

·         In low resolution :    Max. range + delay + gates end + 20 µs

·         In high resolution :   2*( Max. range + delay + gates end) + 20 µs

 

  1. When START In is being used, the maximum time, defined by sum of Max. range + delay + gates end, of any all tests of SLAVE boards must

be lower or equal to the smallest time, defined by sum of Max. range + delay + gates end, of all tests on master MASTER_LINK or MASTER board.

 

  1. GROUNDS MUST BE ALWAYS CONNECTED.

 

Remarks:

 

1.       No START trigger connected between USPC cards:

·         Independent number of tests per MUX when several USPC/MUX are connected

 

2.       With START trigger connected between USPC cards:

·         Same number of test per MUX. Possibility to use 2 MUX with MUX 1 and MUX 2 in full transmission mode.

 

3.       There is a jitter of +/- 20nsec. between  PRF IN and PRF OUT of USPC cards.
